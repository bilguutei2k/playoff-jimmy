    What this can't tell you

    • Dividing by minutes doesn't account for opponents, health, role, pace or workload.
    • This is about how much he scores, not how efficiently, and scoring isn't a player's total impact.
    • The comparison says how unusual a change is among players with real minutes. It doesn't say why it happened, and it isn't evidence of a "clutch" trait.
    • "Close to a coin flip" describes a count, 6 of 11 runs against similar scorers. It isn't a probability model of his seasons.
